3. UX Personas & User Empathy Maps

Designed specifically to solve real physical fatigue, anxiety, and infrastructure gaps

🧓

Col. Sanjeev & Sunita Mehra

Retired Couple (Ages 68 & 64) • Delhi
"We love visiting Rajasthan and Himachal, but driving long hours exhausts our knees and public transport is stressful."
Key Anxieties:

Unclean highway restrooms, staircases at hotels, erratic taxi drivers, physical fatigue.

DeepTrip Solution:

Senior step-in aid, pre-screened hygienic rest stops, ground-floor orchard stays, educated Captain-Guide.

🐕

Pooja & Rohan + 'Max'

Pet Parents (Age 32) • Mumbai
"60% of our holiday plans got cancelled because hotels reject dogs and cab drivers refuse pets."
Key Anxieties:

Cabs refusing pets, leaving dog alone at boarding, unverified accommodations.

DeepTrip Solution:

Owned pet-containment fleet, pet seatbelt harnesses, verified open lawn homestays & 24/7 tele-vet on call.

✨

Vikram & Aditi Singhania

Luxury Family Vacationers • Bengaluru
"We want a seamless luxury holiday with our children without the stress of navigating hill roads."
Key Anxieties:

Aggregator taxi unreliability, bad vehicle hygiene, missing entertainment for kids.

DeepTrip Solution:

BMW 5-Series / Fortuner Legender, Android backseat entertainment with headphones, royal 5-star villas.
